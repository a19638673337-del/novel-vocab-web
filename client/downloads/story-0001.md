# 末班车之前，归还一封信

*A Letter Before the Last Bus*

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
At eight, forty minutes before the last bus, I saw Mr Jiang put an **envelope**（信封） into his toolbox. The old station was closing that night. My **temporary**（临时的） **assignment**（任务） was to **pack**（打包） everything behind the lost-property **counter**（柜台） for **transfer**（转移） to the new office. There was a **deadline**（截止时间） on my checklist and a space for my name below it. There was also an empty rectangle in the **dust**（灰尘） where the letter had been. Mr Jiang had spent the afternoon taking down signs. Now the **elderly**（年长的） man stood beside the exit, his toolbox held against his coat. I asked him to wait.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：八点，距离末班车还有四十分钟时，我看见江师傅把一个信封放进工具箱。旧车站当晚就要关闭。我的临时任务，是把失物招领柜台后面的东西全部装箱，转交新办公室。核对单上写着截止时间，下面留着签名的位置。信原先放着的地方，则在灰尘里留下了一块空白的长方形。江师傅整个下午都在拆指示牌。此刻，这位老人站在出口旁，把工具箱抱在大衣前。我请他等一等。

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
He set the box down beside a **parcel**（包裹） marked **fragile**（易碎的）, moving the parcel first so neither would fall. The letter's **address**（收件地址） said, 'Mr Lamp, **Platform**（站台） Three.' It had been **delivered**（递送） by hand two months earlier. Nobody in our new team knew the name, and our enquiry notice had brought no answer. Its **label**（标签） read: recipient unknown. 'That's me,' he said. His identity card said Jiang Ming. My **instructions**（操作要求） required **proof**（证明） before anyone could **claim**（认领） an item. He touched the **metal**（金属） handle of his box. Beneath his thumb was a faded number: seventeen.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：他把工具箱放在一个标着“易碎”的包裹旁，先挪开包裹，免得两样东西碰倒。信封上的收件地址是：“三号站台，灯先生收。”两个月前，有人亲手把它送来。我们新接手的团队没人认识这个称呼，贴出的认领启事也没有回音。标签上写着：收件人不明。“就是我。”他说。可他的身份证上写的是江明。操作要求规定，任何人认领物品前都得提供证明。他摸着工具箱的金属把手，拇指下面有个褪色的数字：十七。

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
I **hesitated**（犹豫）. I had no **authority**（权限） to give away someone else's letter, but that did not make him a thief. Still, it was easier to **suspect**（怀疑） him than to question my list. Why had he tried to **conceal**（藏起） it? He said he had only just noticed the name while clearing the counter. He had put it somewhere dry for the walk to the bus. 'May I hold it while we check?' I asked. He gave it back without a **conflict**（争执）. I kept it **sealed**（密封着的）. His **explanation**（解释） was possible; my **assumption**（猜测） was not **evidence**（证据）.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：我犹豫了。我无权把别人的信交出去，但这并不等于他就是小偷。尽管如此，怀疑他仍比怀疑我的清单容易。他为什么要把信藏起来？他说，清理柜台时，他才留意到信上的称呼。步行去乘公交车时可能淋雨，他只是想让信保持干燥。“核实期间，先由我保管好吗？”我问。他没有争执，把信还了回来。我没有拆封。他的解释说得通，而我的猜测并不是证据。

---

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
The **register**（登记簿） gave the arrival date but no sender's telephone number. I opened the drawer for another **document**（文件）, a **staff**（员工） list from the station's **previous**（以前的） office. Mr Jiang's name was missing. 'I **retired**（退休） before that list was made,' he said. The number on his box was a **clue**（线索）, though anyone could own an old box. I needed something **reliable**（可信的） to **identify**（确认身份） him. From outside came the engine of a bus returning empty. He looked at the clock. I could finish my **record**（记录） by writing 'unclaimed' again, and let him leave with nothing.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：登记簿里有送达日期，却没有寄信人的电话号码。我打开抽屉，翻出另一份文件：车站从前办公室留下的员工名单。名单里没有江师傅。“做这份名单之前，我就退休了。”他说。工具箱上的编号是一条线索，但谁都可能拥有一个旧箱子。我需要可信的材料来确认他的身份。外面响起一辆空车返站的发动机声。他看了一眼钟。我完全可以再次写下“无人认领”，完成自己的记录，让他两手空空地离开。

---

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
Instead, I called Lin, a **colleague**（同事） who had helped move the older files. She remembered a box of **photographs**（照片） prepared for a local **exhibit**（展览）. It was still **available**（可取用的） in the back room. Mr Jiang offered to help, but I asked him to remain by the counter while I went to **inspect**（检查） it. A **narrow**（狭窄的） passage led between packed chairs. I found an **original**（原始的） duty sheet inside a photograph album. Number seventeen: Jiang Ming, night **repairs**（维修工作）. The handwriting beside it looked oddly **familiar**（熟悉的）. It **matched**（与……相符） the neat labels on half the station's cupboards.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：但我给参与搬运旧档案的同事小林打了电话。她记得，有一箱为当地展览准备的照片，还放在后面的房间里，可以取到。江师傅说要帮忙，我请他留在柜台旁，自己进去查看。打包好的椅子之间留着一条狭窄的通道。我在相册里找到一张原始值班表。十七号：江明，夜间维修。旁边的字迹出奇地熟悉——车站半数柜子上的整齐标签，都是这种写法。

---

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
A photograph showed him beside platform three, holding a lamp above a broken step. Lin told me to call Aunt Qiu, a retired ticket seller. She **recognized**（认出） him in the picture I sent and agreed to act as a **witness**（证人）. 'We called him Mr Lamp,' she said. 'On stormy nights he walked people past that step.' Her words helped **distinguish**（区分） a nickname from a false name. The sheet and photograph were **evidence** I could keep with the claim. Before I ended the call, she **confirmed**（确认） the number without being told it. I felt **ashamed**（羞愧的） of how quickly I had decided he was **guilty**（有罪的）.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：一张照片里，他站在三号站台旁，提着灯照亮一处损坏的台阶。小林让我联系退休售票员邱阿姨。她从我发去的照片里认出了他，也愿意作证。“我们都叫他灯先生。”她说，“暴风雨的晚上，他会带着旅客走过那个台阶。”她的话让我分清了昵称与假名。这张值班表和照片，都是可以附在认领记录里的证据。挂电话前，我没有提示编号，她就准确说出了十七号。我为自己那么快就把他想成有罪的人而羞愧。

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
There was **sufficient**（足够的） proof now. I copied the **relevant**（有关的） page, called my supervisor to **submit**（提交） the details, and received **permission**（许可） to **approve**（批准） the claim. Mr Jiang wrote his name in the space I showed him. 'I owe you an **apology**（道歉）,' I said. 'For taking the letter?' he asked. 'That was my mistake.' 'For deciding what kind of person you were before I checked.' He considered this **particular**（特定的） distinction, then nodded. I would not **blame**（责怪） the instructions for my own thoughts. He would not **insist**（坚持说） that knowing the letter was his meant he could simply take it. We had both stopped defending ourselves.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：现在，证明已经足够了。我复印相关页，打电话向主管提交情况，获准办理认领。江师傅在我指的位置写下名字。“我欠您一个道歉。”我说。“因为拿信的事？”他问，“那是我不对。”“是因为我还没查清，就先认定了您是什么样的人。”他想了想这两者之间的区别，点点头。我不会把自己的想法推给操作要求；他也不再坚持说，既然知道信是自己的，就可以直接拿走。我们都不再忙着为自己辩解。

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
He opened the letter himself. Inside were several sheets of paper and a second envelope **containing**（装有） sixty yuan. 'Dear Mr Lamp,' he read, then stopped. 'Mei?' He said the name as a question, and the letter began to **reveal**（揭示） a night I had never known. Fifteen years earlier, a sixteen-year-old girl had reached the station without enough money to go home. She had waited for hours, **reluctant**（不情愿的） to ask anyone. Mr Jiang had found her a warm **shelter**（避寒处） in the waiting room, brought her food from the staff's emergency food **supply**（储备）, and paid the missing **fare**（车费）. This money was a **debt**（欠款） she had never forgotten.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：他亲手拆开了信。里面是几页信纸，还有一个装着六十元的小信封。“亲爱的灯先生——”他读到这里停住了。“小梅？”他说出这个名字时，语气里带着疑问。信揭开了一个我从未听说过的夜晚。十五年前，一个十六岁的女孩来到车站，身上的钱不够回家。她等了几个小时，不愿开口求人。江师傅在候车室里给她找了个暖和的避寒处，从员工储备的食物里拿了些吃的，又替她补足车费。她一直记得这笔欠款。

---

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
With his **consent**（同意）, I read the next page aloud. Mei had **feared**（害怕） he would **demand**（要求） an explanation for leaving home. Instead, he had helped her telephone her grandmother, then offered to **accompany**（陪同） her to the bus. 'You didn't ask me to **confess**（坦白） anything,' she wrote. 'You asked which step I couldn't see.' She had **eventually**（终于） found his nickname in an online station photograph and delivered this letter during a visit. She did not know his real name. He had not worked here for years; he had returned today only to **repair**（修理） a cupboard before the move. Their long **delay**（耽搁） suddenly made sense.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：征得他同意后，我朗读了下一页。小梅当时害怕他会追问自己为什么离家。他却只是帮她给外婆打电话，然后陪她去乘车。“你没有要我坦白什么。”她写道，“你只是问我，看不清哪一级台阶。”后来，她终于在网上的一张车站旧照片里找到了这个昵称，趁着一次回乡，亲自送来了信。她不知道他的真名。他也早已不在这里工作；今天回来，只是想在搬迁前修好一个柜子。信为什么迟迟未能交到手里，终于说得通了。

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
The final page was a **proposal**（提议）. Mei now helped run a **community**（社区） reading room near the bus's last stop. The **residents**（居民） had gathered books, but the children's shelves needed work. Would he **volunteer**（自愿帮忙） there one evening? She had **enclosed**（随信附上） a small map and her telephone number. 'That's a polite way to give an old man something to do,' he said. I saw a different **possibility**（可能性）. Someone who had once accepted help was asking for his again. I could not **guarantee**（保证） how the meeting would go, but I could **respond**（回复） to the invitation. I offered him the phone.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：最后一页是个提议。小梅如今帮忙经营一间社区阅览室，就在这趟公交车终点站附近。居民们凑齐了书，孩子们的书架却还需要修整。她问他，是否愿意找个晚上来帮忙？信里附着一张小地图和电话号码。“这是客气地给老头儿找点事干。”他说。我看见的却是另一种可能：一个曾经受过帮助的人，再次需要他的本领。我无法保证重逢会是什么样子，但至少可以回复这份邀请。我把电话递给他。

---

<!-- vocab-body:start -->
He was still uncertain. 'She may not remember my face.' I did not try to **persuade**（说服） him that everything would be easy. I dialled only after he nodded. A woman answered, with chairs scraping in the background. He **introduced**（介绍） himself as Jiang Ming, and silence followed. Then he added, 'The man with the lamp.' Her **response**（回应） was a sharp breath. 'Are you coming?' she asked. He looked at his tools. 'I can **restore**（修复） a shelf,' he said, almost in a **whisper**（低声）. My own **concern**（担心） about the checklist grew smaller. Through the window, I checked the **departure**（发车） **board**（显示牌）: 20:40. The clock below it read 20:33.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：他仍拿不准。“她也许不记得我的脸了。”我没有试图说服他一切都会顺利。等他点了头，我才拨号。电话那头，一个女人伴着搬椅子的声音接起来。他介绍自己叫江明，那边安静了片刻。他又补充：“提灯的那个。”她猛地吸了一口气。“您要来吗？”她问。他看着自己的工具。“我会修书架。”他的声音很轻。我对核对单的担心也变得不那么重要了。我透过窗户查看发车牌：二十点四十分。下面的钟指着二十点三十三分。

We had seven minutes. I packed the remaining objects according to the **arrangement**（安排） for the move, keeping each item in its **proper**（正确的） box. Nothing would be thrown away because a room was closing. Mr Jiang helped me **secure**（固定好） the lid of the fragile parcel. The last **entry**（条目） on my list now read 'collected by recipient', with the supporting pages **attached**（附上）. I left the album out for the local archive to **preserve**（保存）. Lin arrived to take over, checking each box before accepting it. Her **cooperation**（配合） gave us time to leave. My name could wait until the work was **complete**（完成的）; now it was.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：还剩七分钟。我按搬迁安排，把剩余物品分别装入对应的箱子。不能因为一间屋子要关闭，就把东西扔掉。江师傅帮我固定好易碎包裹的箱盖。清单最后一项，如今写着“收件人已领取”，后面附着证明材料。我把相册单独留出，准备交给地方档案馆保存。小林来接班，逐一核对箱子后接收。她的配合，让我们来得及离开。工作完成之后才该签下我的名字；现在，已经完成了。

Outside, rain had made the **path**（小路） shine. Mr Jiang carried the toolbox, and I carried the lamp we had used behind the counter. At the broken **edge**（边缘） of the pavement he put out one hand to guide me, a movement so **ordinary**（寻常的） that I nearly missed it. We reached the bus as the driver began his final check. I felt **relief**（如释重负）, but Mr Jiang was looking **beyond**（越过） me, toward the dark road and its **destination**（目的地）. 'Keep the light for the return walk,' he said. Then he climbed **aboard**（上车）. This time, I watched him put the letter in his toolbox and did not ask him to stop.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：外面，雨把路面洗得发亮。江师傅提着工具箱，我提着柜台后刚才用过的灯。经过人行道一处破损的边缘时，他伸手引我过去。这个动作太寻常，我险些没有留意。我们赶到时，司机正做发车前的最后检查。我松了一口气，江师傅却望过我的肩头，看向黑暗中的道路和它通往的目的地。“灯留着，回来走路用。”他说，随后上了车。这一次，我看着他把信放进工具箱，没有再叫他停下。

At half past nine, Lin showed me a message from Mei, who had asked for our office number during the call. The photograph showed Mr Jiang kneeling beside a shelf while two children held the **boards**（木板）. He was giving them an **explanation**（说明） with one finger raised. Behind him, Mei was smiling. I had **expected**（预料） the station's last night to **represent**（意味着） an ending: a locked door, an empty room, a **permanent**（永久的） silence. Instead, here was a small **connection**（联系） that had **survived**（延续下来） the move. I set the lamp on the counter and turned it off. The letter had reached someone. So had the man who carried it.
<!-- vocab-body:end -->

中文对照：九点半，小林给我看小梅发来的消息。刚才通话时，她问了办公室的号码。照片里，江师傅跪在书架旁，两个孩子替他扶着木板。他竖起一根手指，正向他们讲解。身后的小梅笑着。我原以为，车站的最后一夜意味着结束：一扇锁上的门，一间空屋子，一场永久的寂静。可眼前，有一份微小的联系，经受住了搬迁，延续了下来。我把灯放在柜台上，关掉了它。那封信找到了等它的人。带着信的人，也一样。
